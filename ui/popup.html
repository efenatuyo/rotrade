RoTrade - Settings

Open a Roblox tab to sync account-specific options.

Common owners

Parameters for finding users who own items you trade for.

Trade history

How long sent trades are remembered.

Trade list

Per-row decorations on the Roblox trade list pages (inbound, outbound, completed, inactive).

Adds a corner box showing your-side total vs their-side total (color-coded glow bar).

Trade summary

Win/loss chips shown between trade offers on a trade detail.

Show the RAP, Value and $ chips between offers on the trade detail.

Roli badges

Show Rolimons badges (Rolibadges) next to the user info on a Roblox profile.

When off, no badges are added to the user profile header.

User profile metric

Which Rolimons number to show on user profile pages (next to the friend/follower row).

Pick which total appears as the inventory chip on /users/<id>/profile.

Robux → USD

How many US dollars one thousand Robux is worth for estimates (trade totals, catalog USD rows, reseller listings). Default 4 is a common reference rate.

When off, USD rows are hidden everywhere (catalog, trade detail, partner inventory).

Notifications

Toast notifications and sound when trades change status (accepted, countered, declined, expired).

When off, no toast or sound is shown for trade status changes. Native OS notifications even when the Roblox tab isn't focused. Requires the Notifications permission (you'll be prompted the first time you enable this).

Auto 2FA

Store your authenticator secret for auto-confirm when sending trades (encrypted).
